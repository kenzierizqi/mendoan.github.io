Resep Asli Banyumas Authentic Banyumas Recipe

Resep Tempe Mendoan Asli Banyumas (Bilingual) Authentic Banyumas Tempeh Mendoan Recipe

Bahan Utama Main Ingredients

  • 10 lembar tempe mendoan khusus (atau tempe biasa yang diiris setipis mungkin)
  • 250 gram tepung terigu protein sedang
  • 2 sendok makan tepung beras (untuk memberi sedikit tekstur pada adonan)
  • 400 ml air bersih
  • 2 batang daun bawang, diiris halus
  • Minyak goreng secukupnya (harus banyak agar tempe terendam sepenuhnya)
  • 10 sheets of special mendoan tempeh (or regular tempeh sliced as thinly as possible)
  • 250 grams medium-protein wheat flour
  • 2 tablespoons rice flour (to give the batter a slight texture)
  • 400 ml clean water
  • 2 stalks of scallions, finely sliced
  • Cooking oil as needed (must be plenty so the tempeh is fully submerged)

Bumbu Halus Ground Spices

  • 3 siung bawang putih
  • 2 siung bawang merah
  • 1 sendok teh biji ketumbar
  • 1 ruas jari kencur — kunci aroma mendoan otentik
  • Garam dan kaldu bubuk secukupnya
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 cloves of shallots
  • 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of aromatic ginger (kencur)
  • Salt and broth powder to taste

Cara Memasak Cooking Steps

  1. Haluskan bawang putih, bawang merah, ketumbar, kencur, garam, dan kaldu bubuk.
  2. Dalam wadah besar, campurkan tepung terigu, tepung beras, bumbu halus, dan air. Aduk rata hingga tidak ada gumpalan.
  3. Masukkan irisan daun bawang ke dalam adonan, aduk rata.
  4. Panaskan minyak goreng dalam jumlah banyak (deep frying) hingga benar-benar panas.
  5. Celupkan tempe mendoan ke dalam adonan tepung hingga terbalut rata.
  6. Goreng tempe dalam minyak panas selama 2–4 menit saja (setengah matang/lembut). Jangan sampai kering atau crispy.
  7. Angkat dan tiriskan. Sajikan hangat dengan sambal kecap dan cabai rawit hijau.
  1. Grind garlic, shallots, coriander, kencur, salt, and broth powder into a smooth paste.
  2. In a large bowl, mix wheat flour, rice flour, ground spices, and water. Stir well until there are no lumps.
  3. Add the sliced scallions into the batter and mix well.
  4. Heat a generous amount of cooking oil in a pan (deep frying) until very hot.
  5. Dip the mendoan tempeh into the batter until evenly coated.
  6. Fry the tempeh in the hot oil for only 2–4 minutes (half-cooked/soft). Do not fry until dry or crispy.
  7. Remove and drain. Serve hot with sweet soy sauce chili and fresh green bird's eye chilies.
